About this guide

A free, non-profit introduction to Nepal, made by one of its own.

Visit Nepal began with a simple wish: to share the country I come from with people who have never been there, and to help them arrive prepared, curious and respectful.

Nepal is often known for one mountain. It is also temples that are still in daily use, crafts passed down through families, a calendar of its own and more than a hundred communities with their own languages and beliefs. This guide tries to bring that range together in one calm, easy place.

What you will find here

A practical guide

Where to go, the best months, sample trips, visas, permits, altitude and etiquette.

Living information

News, the Nepali calendar and daily rashifal links update on their own, and a tourist map shows where everything is.

Culture and craft

The beliefs, festivals and handmade arts that give Nepal its character.

How this site works

  • It is non-profit. There is no advertising, nothing to buy and no income of any kind.
  • It never asks for or collects money. The flood relief link goes straight to the Government of Nepal.
  • The site itself does not track visitors or ask for personal details.
  • Facts are checked against official and reputable sources, and news links always open the original publisher.
  • Photographs are used under their free licences and credited below.

A note on accuracy

Rules for visas and permits change, and festival dates follow the moon. Please confirm important details with the Department of Immigration or the Nepal Tourism Board before you travel.
