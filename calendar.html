Nepali calendar

Nepal counts its years in Bikram Sambat, about 56 years and 8 months ahead of the Western calendar. The new year begins in mid April.

Today's rashifal

Choose your sign to read today's reading on Hamro Patro, Nepal's most used calendar. It updates every morning on its own. Not sure of your sign? In Nepal your rashi comes from the Moon at your birth, and many names begin with one of your sign's letters, shown on each card.
