Places to go

Six destinations, from the temples of Kathmandu to the high desert of Mustang.

Kathmandu Valley Everest region Pokhara and the Annapurnas Chitwan National Park Lumbini Muktinath and Mustang

काठमाडौं उपत्यका

Kathmandu Valley

Best time
October to April
Stay
2 to 3 days
Getting there
International flights to Kathmandu

The Kathmandu Valley holds seven groups of monuments that together form a single World Heritage Site: the old royal squares of Kathmandu, Patan and Bhaktapur, the stupas of Swayambhunath and Boudhanath, and the temples of Pashupatinath and Changu Narayan.

Pashupatinath, the great temple of Shiva, spreads along both banks of the holy Bagmati River. Its gilded pagoda roof and silver doors are among the finest in Nepal. Only Hindus may enter the main courtyard, but everyone is welcome in the wider complex, and the evening aarti on the riverbank is unforgettable. Cremations take place on the ghats, so watch quietly and do not photograph grieving families.

Swayambhunath looks down on the city from a wooded hill, its painted eyes gazing in four directions. Boudhanath, one of the largest stupas in the world, is the heart of Tibetan Buddhist life in Kathmandu. Walk around it clockwise at dusk, with the pilgrims and their prayer wheels.

सगरमाथा

Everest region

Best time
October to November, March to May
Stay
12 to 16 days
Permits
National park and local area permits, licensed guide

Mount Everest, Sagarmatha in Nepali and Chomolungma in Tibetan, rises 8,848.86 metres on the border between Nepal and Tibet. That figure was agreed by Nepal and China in 2020 after new surveys by both countries.

You do not need to climb it to feel its size. The trek to Base Camp begins with a short mountain flight to Lukla and follows the Dudh Koshi valley through Sherpa villages, prayer walls and forests of Sagarmatha National Park. Namche Bazaar, the Sherpa capital, is the place to rest and adjust to the altitude. Tengboche Monastery sits beneath the peak of Ama Dablam.

Most trekkers climb Kala Patthar, a rocky viewpoint above Gorak Shep, for the closest look at the summit. Take the climb slowly. Altitude sickness can affect anyone.

पोखरा र अन्नपूर्ण

Pokhara and the Annapurnas

Best time
October to November, March to May
Stay
3 to 18 days
Getting there
A 25 minute flight or a day by road from Kathmandu

Pokhara is Nepal's lakeside town, a relaxed base with Phewa Lake at its feet and the fishtail peak of Machhapuchhre above. Watch sunrise from Sarangkot, walk up to the World Peace Pagoda, or simply rest after a trek.

The Annapurnas offer treks for every level. Poon Hill is a gentle four or five days through stone villages and rhododendron forest. The Annapurna Circuit is a classic of two weeks or more, crossing the Thorong La at 5,416 metres.

From Manang, a side trip of three or four days leads to Tilicho Lake. At 4,919 metres it is often called the highest lake of its size in the world, fed by glaciers from the north face of the Annapurnas. Many Hindu pilgrims believe it is a holy lake described in the Ramayana.

चितवन राष्ट्रिय निकुञ्ज

Chitwan National Park

Best time
October to March
Stay
2 to 3 days
Getting there
Tourist bus or a short flight to Bharatpur

Founded in 1973 as Nepal's first national park and a World Heritage Site since 1984, Chitwan protects about 952 square kilometres of grassland and forest in the warm Inner Terai. Together with parks in Nepal and India next door, it forms one of the most important tiger habitats in Asia.

Walk the jungle with an experienced guide, take a jeep safari, or drift down the Rapti River by dugout canoe looking for gharial crocodiles. The park is one of the last strongholds of the one-horned rhino, and birdwatchers can spot more than 500 species. In the villages around Sauraha, the Tharu people share their food, dance and culture.

Chitwan district lies downstream of the rivers hit by the August 2026 flood. Check road and park conditions before you travel.

लुम्बिनी

Lumbini

Best time
October to March
Stay
1 to 2 days
Getting there
Fly to Gautam Buddha International Airport, Bhairahawa

Lumbini is where Queen Mayadevi gave birth to Siddhartha Gautama, who became the Buddha. Nepali tradition dates the birth to 623 BCE, while many historians place it somewhat later. It has been a World Heritage Site since 1997.

The Maya Devi Temple stands over foundations more than 2,000 years old, beside the sacred pond where the queen is said to have bathed. Next to it rises the Ashoka Pillar, raised by the Indian emperor when he came here as a pilgrim in 249 BCE.

Beyond the sacred garden, the monastic zone designed by the Japanese architect Kenzo Tange holds temples built by Buddhist communities from around the world. Hire a bicycle, visit a few, and find a quiet place to sit.

मुक्तिनाथ

Muktinath and Mustang

Altitude
3,710 m
Stay
3 to 5 days
Getting there
Fly Pokhara to Jomsom, then jeep

Muktinath sits beneath the Thorong La in the high, dry valley of Mustang. Hindus call it Mukti Kshetra, the place of liberation, and count it among the most sacred temples of Vishnu. Buddhists know it as Chumig Gyatsa, the hundred waters, and say Guru Rinpoche meditated here.

Pilgrims bathe beneath 108 bull head spouts of icy water around the main temple. Nearby, in the small Jwala Mai temple, a natural gas flame burns beside a spring, so fire and water appear together.

The Kali Gandaki valley below is the source of shaligram stones, fossils worshipped as forms of Vishnu. The altitude is high, so allow time to adjust, and avoid rushing up by helicopter.
